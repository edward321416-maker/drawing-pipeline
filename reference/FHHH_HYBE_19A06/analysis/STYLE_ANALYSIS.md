# HYBE architectural-plan style research — preliminary

PROVENANCE
- FHHH original 19A06 webpage: project and design-team text verified. The full original photo/drawing gallery has not been captured.
- COM office 8–16F plan, 19F floor plan and canteen furniture diagram: previewed in web image search. ArchDaily/COM narrative states 500x500 mm flexible furniture grid. The 0.5m grid belongs to HYBE, not a mandatory geometry module for our market.
- Every numeric style token here is an independent PROPOSAL, NOT a measurement from the originals.

OBSERVATIONS FROM COM VISUALS
1. Strong black structural/core poche; wall and column symbols legible at a glance.
2. Work/furniture modules organized into controlled clusters rather than packed to maximum count.
3. Clear white circulation separating pale gray functional groups.
4. Muted green at tree/landscape areas rather than dense black tree-branch noise.
5. Small occasional blue circled program markers paired with a clean legend and a scale bar.
6. Negative space, light furniture lines and an obvious hierarchy of black/gray/color.

ADAPTATION TO OUR 1F MODEL
- Retain approved M01–M06/P01–P04/S01–S04/C01–C04 functional identities.
- M02: organized few retail clusters with generous M06 circulation.
- M03: larger pop-up/demo islands, distinct from M02 grid.
- M04/M05: visibly different counter vs table/seating topologies.
- P01: reserve white public path. P03: pale green and simplified tree crowns.
- C02: clear plinth/discussion sequence; entry remains UNVERIFIED_MODEL_OPENING.
- Keep actual structure/columns/core/T1–T4 unchanged and continue to reconcile 34 vs 23 displayed columns.
- At 1:500 selectively remove unnecessary stipple/branches and reduce wobble_ring noise in decorative layer only; NEVER global simplify structural curves.
- Single style authority: adapted tokens JSON, not independently modified hardcoded lineweights.

LIMITATIONS
